name: AVA conversion flow
description: User's conversion goal and distinction between public trial and campaign offers
---
Visitors must understand the product before being asked for email. The user requested offer button → email → download, with actual AVA product shots.

**Why:** The user said upfront email capture is confusing because it does not explain the product.

**How to apply:** Do not auto-open the email gate on landing. Keep product benefits and screenshots visible before commitment.

Use one reusable landing page personalized by configured influencer UTMs, rather than maintaining separate pages per influencer.

**Why:** The user wants individual influencer customization that can be automated.

**How to apply:** Keep a central approved campaign list; do not let arbitrary URL text establish an offer or imply an endorsement.

The user's mention of six months free was an example, not confirmed campaign terms. Public AVA copy inspected during this build advertised 14 days free then $12.99/month. The SEANVIP-specific offer remains unconfirmed.

**Why:** A landing-page headline alone cannot grant an App Store subscription promotion.

**How to apply:** Confirm the campaign's actual entitlement and redemption mechanism before advertising a different free period. Local lead capture is not a connection to AVA's existing mailing system.

AVA gifting should speak to partners giving mothers relief from the mental load. The user clarified that the gifting page is design only for their developer; payment implementation is not required.

**Why:** The user wants giving AVA to feel like practical support for a happier mom and home.

**How to apply:** Keep gift positioning distinct from the ordinary free-trial flow. Deliver the intended gifting design with clearly labeled prototype interactions, not payment integration or public “coming soon” messaging. Do not represent a download as paid gift access.
