Visitors must understand the product before being asked for email. The user requested offer button → email → download, with actual AVA product shots.

**Why:** The user said upfront email capture is confusing because it does not explain the product.

**How to apply:** Do not auto-open the email gate on landing. Keep product benefits and screenshots visible before commitment.

The user's mention of six months free was an example, not confirmed campaign terms. Public AVA copy inspected during this build advertised 14 days free then $12.99/month. The SEANVIP-specific offer remains unconfirmed.

**Why:** A landing-page headline alone cannot grant an App Store subscription promotion.

**How to apply:** Confirm the campaign's actual entitlement and redemption mechanism before advertising a different free period. Local lead capture is not a connection to AVA's existing mailing system.
